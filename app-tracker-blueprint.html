App Tracker — Implementation Blueprint

Backstage · PostgreSQL · Keycloak · GitLab · MS Teams  |  March 2026 · v1.1

1. Overview
Purpose

Replaces manual PMO coordination (email, Excel) with a structured, automated, org-wide delivery governance platform. Single source of truth for all service deployments across environments. Built for PMO team as primary users.

Core concepts
Entry — one service + env + version (upsert key)
Sign-off — approval from a role's assigned EM/lead
Auto-status — derived from sign-offs automatically
Display status — manual override ?? auto-status
Scope — global or app-family-group (for Dev roles)
